## Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how LoanServ ("LoanServ", "we", "us" or "our"), operating the website **loanserv.in**, collects, uses, shares and safeguards your personal information. LoanServ is a Direct Selling Agent (DSA) and loan facilitator based in Hyderabad, Telangana. We are **not a lender, bank or NBFC**. We help connect prospective borrowers with our partner banks and Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs), who take all lending decisions independently.

By using loanserv.in or submitting any information through our forms, you consent to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the website or share your information with us.

## Information We Collect

We collect only the information we need to help you explore loan options and to operate our website. This includes:

- **Contact and identity details** you enter into our apply-lead form: your name, mobile number, email address, city, employment type and the loan amount or product you are interested in.
- **Credit-check details** submitted with your explicit consent for our free credit-score feature: your PAN and date of birth. These are handled as described in our separate [Data & Privacy Policy (CIBIL / Credit Score)](/legal/data-policy).
- **Chat messages** you type into our AI chat assistant, and the contact and loan details you enter into its callback form (see "AI Chat Assistant" below).
- **Technical and usage data** collected automatically: IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, referring URLs and time spent on pages. When you submit a form, we also record how you reached our site (for example, a search engine, an advertisement or a partner's referral link) along with your enquiry.
- **Cookie-based data** gathered through our own and third-party cookies (see below and our [Cookie Policy](/legal/cookie-policy)).

We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18 years of age.

## Cookies, Google Analytics and Advertising

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to function correctly, understand traffic and display advertising.

- **Essential cookies** keep the site working, remember your form progress and maintain security.
- **Analytics cookies** are set through **Google Analytics**, which helps us understand how visitors use loanserv.in so we can improve it. Google Analytics collects aggregated, largely pseudonymous usage statistics.
- **Advertising cookies** are set through **Google AdSense** and other **third-party ad vendors**. Google and its partners use cookies (including the DoubleClick/`__gads` family) to serve ads based on your prior visits to our and other websites. These vendors may use device identifiers to deliver and measure interest-based advertising.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings and can opt out of personalised Google advertising via Google's Ads Settings. Full details, cookie categories and opt-out links are in our [Cookie Policy](/legal/cookie-policy).

## AI Chat Assistant

Our website offers an optional chat assistant that answers general questions about loans, EMIs, credit scores and our service. Please note:

- **It is an automated AI system, not a person.** Its answers are general information drawn from the content on loanserv.in. They may be incomplete or inaccurate, and they are not financial, legal or tax advice, a loan offer or an approval.
- **Your messages are processed by an AI provider.** To generate replies, the messages you type are sent to **Sarvam AI** (sarvam.ai), which processes them on our behalf as our service provider.
- **We remove identifiers first.** Before any message is sent to the AI provider, we automatically remove mobile numbers, email addresses, PAN, Aadhaar and card numbers, and long account-style numbers. Please do not share Aadhaar, PAN, bank account details, OTPs or passwords in the chat.
- **Your contact details are not sent to the AI provider.** If you ask for a callback, the name, mobile number and email you enter in the callback form come directly to us. Loan details you mentioned in the chat (such as amount or city) may be suggested in the form for you to check.
- **We do not keep chat history on our servers.** A conversation lasts only while your browser tab is open. If you request a callback, a copy of the conversation (with the identifiers above removed) is attached to your enquiry so our advisor understands your requirement. It is then kept and handled like any other enquiry, as described in this policy.
- **Callbacks need your consent.** We contact you only if you tick the consent box in the callback form. This is the same consent as our apply form, including an automated AI voice callback.

Using the chat is optional; you can always reach us through the apply form, phone, WhatsApp or email instead.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Pass your loan enquiry to relevant partner banks and NBFCs so they can evaluate and contact you.
- Provide the free, consent-based credit-score check you request.
- Respond to your queries, calls and support requests, including through our AI chat assistant.
- Understand which channels (search, advertising, partners) bring enquiries to us.
- Display **indicative** rates, eligibility and offers relevant to you. Indicative figures are illustrative only and are not an offer or approval.
- Operate, secure, analyse and improve the website.
- Comply with applicable law and lawful requests.

## How We Share Leads with Partner Banks and NBFCs

The core purpose of LoanServ is facilitation. When you submit the apply-lead form, **you authorise us to share your enquiry details with our partner banks and NBFCs** and their authorised representatives, so that they may assess your eligibility and contact you regarding loan products.

Once a lead is shared, the partner lender processes your information under **its own privacy policy and terms**, over which LoanServ has no control. Loan approval, interest rates, charges and final terms are determined **solely at the discretion of the partner bank or NBFC**. We may also share data with service providers who host our site, provide analytics and advertising, or provide AI services (such as Sarvam AI, which powers our chat assistant), and with authorities where legally required. We do **not** sell your personal information.

## Data Retention

We retain lead and contact information only for as long as necessary to facilitate your enquiry, to allow partner lenders to follow up, and to meet legal or record-keeping obligations. Consent-based credit-check inputs such as PAN and date of birth are not stored longer than needed to perform the requested check, in line with our [Data & Privacy Policy](/legal/data-policy). Aggregated analytics data may be retained for longer as it does not identify you directly.

## Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures, including HTTPS/TLS encryption for data in transit, to protect your information against unauthorised access, loss or misuse. However, no method of internet transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Your Rights

Subject to applicable Indian law, you may:

- Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
- Request correction of inaccurate or outdated information.
- Withdraw consent for the credit-score check or marketing contact at any time.
- Request deletion of your information where we are not required to retain it.
- Object to certain processing, including interest-based advertising.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We will respond within a reasonable time.
